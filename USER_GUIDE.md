# GT Driving Website User Guide

This public guide explains how learners can use the GT Driving website. Use the
official website address published by GT Driving or supplied in your booking
confirmation.

## Explore the website

The public website includes information about GT Driving, lesson packages,
instructors, testimonials, and lesson availability.

## Book a driving lesson

1. Open the booking calendar on the home page.
2. Choose an instructor.
3. Select an available date and time.
4. Enter the requested learner and lesson information.
5. Review the booking summary and displayed fee.
6. Submit the booking and retain the confirmation for your records.

Only provide information needed for the lesson. Do not enter passwords,
payment-card details, identity documents, or other secrets in the lesson notes.

## Use a learner account

After signing in, open **Dashboard** from the account menu. Learners can review
their lessons and use the controls displayed for bookings that are eligible for
changes.

Availability and permitted changes depend on the booking's timing and status.
If the required option is not displayed, contact GT Driving through its
published support channel.

## Account security

- Use a unique password that is not used for another service.
- Enable multi-factor authentication when the option is available.
- Keep sign-in codes and account-recovery information private.
- Sign out when using a shared device.
- Use the password-reset link on the sign-in screen if you cannot access your
  account.

GT Driving representatives should not ask for your password or authentication
codes.

## Troubleshooting

- **A time is unavailable:** choose another available time or instructor.
- **A booking option is unavailable:** contact GT Driving through its published
  support channel.
- **Changes do not appear:** refresh the page and sign in again if prompted.
- **An account email does not arrive:** check the spam folder, confirm the
  address entered is correct, and use the resend option if one is displayed.

## Staff procedures

Staff access, privileged-account details, administrative addresses, internal
configuration, and data-management procedures are intentionally excluded from
this public guide. Authorised staff should use the privately maintained staff
handbook.
